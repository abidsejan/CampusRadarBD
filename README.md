# CampusRadar BD

**Discover university events and student opportunities across Bangladesh — all in one place.**

CampusRadar BD is a web platform designed to bring university events and student opportunities in Bangladesh into one searchable, structured catalogue. Instead of relying on scattered social media posts, club groups, posters, university websites, and messaging apps, students can find event information in one place. Organizers can publish detailed event pages, while administrators help keep information accurate and trustworthy.

## Overview

University events and student opportunities are often announced across multiple platforms. Important information—such as eligibility, team size, fees, deadlines, schedules, and registration links—may be missing or difficult to find. Students outside the organizing institution can also miss opportunities, and outdated posts may remain visible after an event deadline, venue change, or cancellation.

CampusRadar BD aims to make event discovery clearer and more accessible by providing structured event listings, useful search and filtering options, detailed event information, and moderation to support reliable listings.

## Problem Statement

CampusRadar BD is designed to address these common challenges:

- **Scattered announcements:** Event information is spread across social media, club groups, posters, university websites, and messaging apps.
- **Missing details:** Announcements may not include eligibility rules, team-size requirements, fees, deadlines, or reliable registration links.
- **Limited reach:** Students outside the host university may not hear about an opportunity in time.
- **Repeated questions:** Organizers often have to answer the same questions individually.
- **Outdated information:** Old announcements may remain visible after deadlines, venue changes, or cancellations.

## Objectives

- Make upcoming university events searchable by category, date, location, institution, eligibility, and registration status.
- Give each event a detailed page so students can make informed participation decisions.
- Help organizers publish structured event information and reach students beyond their existing networks.
- Keep public event publishing subject to moderation.
- Build trust through organizer verification, official registration links, accurate event statuses, and report handling.

## Key Features

### For Students

- **Centralized event discovery:** Explore university events and student opportunities from different institutions in one catalogue.
- **Search and filtering:** Find events by category, date, location, institution, eligibility, and registration status.
- **Detailed event pages:** Review event descriptions, dates, venues, images, schedules, eligibility rules, segments, and official registration links.
- **Deadline awareness:** Find key dates and registration information more easily.
- **Cross-university access:** Discover opportunities beyond your own university.
- **Clear event status:** Help students distinguish current listings from events that have changed or are no longer active.

### For Organizers and Clubs

- **Structured event publishing:** Submit event information in a consistent format.
- **Detailed event pages:** Include schedules, venues, images, eligibility criteria, segments, fees, deadlines, and official registration links where applicable.
- **Wider reach:** Make opportunities easier to discover beyond existing followers and club communities.
- **Fewer repeated questions:** Present essential information together in one place.

### For Universities and Departments

- **Greater visibility:** Showcase academic, technical, cultural, and community activities.
- **Cross-institution discovery:** Help students from other institutions find relevant opportunities.
- **Consistent information:** Present event details in a structured format.

### For Platform Administrators

- **Moderation:** Review submissions before public publishing.
- **Organizer verification:** Support confidence in the source of event announcements.
- **Event status management:** Help keep listings accurate when deadlines, venues, or event plans change.
- **Report handling:** Investigate reports and help maintain a trustworthy catalogue.
- **Consistent metadata:** Keep event information organized and comparable.

> **Note:** These features describe the project's intended scope. Their availability in the current application depends on implementation progress.

## How It Helps

2. Organizing important details into consistent event pages.
3. Helping students discover opportunities across institutions.

5. Supporting reliable listings through moderation, verification, and status updates.
